- (decision) Product: multi-tool dashboard/web app for understanding price formation in the Nordic power market, expandable to wider Europe later.
- (decision) Element 1 (core/MVP): top-down fundamental short-term forecast for Nordic system price, using historical aggregated NEMO bid curves adjusted for fundamentals changes since baseline (wind, solar, RoR hydro, must-run, water values, thermal SRMCs, availabilities, net export flows). Special techniques: baseline curve lead-time selection, regression-based water value change derivation.
- (decision) Element 2: statistical model for forward periods (DEC, Q4, CAL27, etc.) driven by changes in inflow forecasts, wind forecasts, availabilities, fuels (gas, coal, EUA).
- (decision) Both elements 1 and 2 benchmarked against financial market forward/forward curve expectations (short-term and long-term contracts).
- (decision) Future expansion (explicitly later-phase, not v1): area prices across wider Europe, requiring flow-based clearing and techniques to dissolve aggregated bid curves into area curves incl. attributing bids to areas for correct water-value assignment.
- (decision) Possible third element (later-phase): a news synthesizer explaining price changes by combining news with fundamental data model observations.
- (decision) Primary users: user + professional industry colleagues (traders/hedgers), i.e. real trading-desk utility is the design bar, even though this is delivered as an IBE160 (Høgskolen i Molde, autumn 2026) group course project.
- (decision) Stack: full stack web app. No specific framework/language mandated by user.
